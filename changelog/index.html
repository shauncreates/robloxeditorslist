Updates to Roblox Editors List.

Unreleased · Prepared 15 October 2026

Accounts and applications

  • Email-confirmed accounts, password recovery and usernames.
  • A separate account page for editing your linked editor profile and requesting a profile link.
  • Application styles that follow the offered editing styles, plus Other.
  • Final approval and rejection decisions with a private record of the reviewer and reason.
  • Approved applications stay private until a List Contributor converts them into editor profiles.
  • Automatic profile and ranking saves, live refresh and smoother profile dropdowns.
  • Rankings stored in Supabase, with checks to prevent outdated saves from replacing newer changes.
