Changelog

Updates to Roblox Editors List.

19 October 2026

Clans redesign

  • Search clans by name or prefix, and sort the directory by name or member count.
  • Clan pages now have separate Rankings, Members and management tabs, with member counts and editor pictures.
  • Clan rankings show #1, #2, #3 within the clan, following the overall list's order. Choosing an editing style uses that style's order.
  • Clan prefixes now sit before editor names on the main rankings list, including on mobile.
  • Improved clan layouts for smaller screens, keyboard navigation and long names.

18 October 2026

Profile pictures and privacy

  • Fixed fetching TikTok profile pictures for editor profiles.
  • Expanded the Privacy and YouTube connection notices to explain what REL reads, how it uses that information and how to disconnect.
  • Added a short explanation of optional YouTube linking on the homepage.

17 October 2026

Social connection fixes and editor linking

  • Fixed errors that prevented Google and TikTok connections from completing.
  • A confirmed TikTok connection can automatically link your REL account to an existing editor with the same TikTok username, when that profile is available to link.
  • When staff add an approved applicant to the list, a matching confirmed TikTok connection can link the applicant's account to their new editor profile.
  • Owner-reviewed editor linking remains available.

16 October 2026

Application tracking, social connections and clans

  • Apply using a confirmed REL account, then follow your application's status and review message in My account.
  • Added application receipt emails and approval or denial emails with the review message.
  • Reviewed applications leave the active review queue. Owners can archive them, and staff can see who reviewed applications or changed rankings.
  • Added optional TikTok and Google-authorised YouTube connections to confirm ownership of a social account. These connections do not sign you into REL.
  • Ranked editors can appear on separate Popularity lists for TikTok followers and YouTube subscribers. Counts show when they were last updated; reconnect to refresh them.
  • Editors can choose TikTok or YouTube as their main platform for channel links and profile-picture fetching.
  • Create a clan with a four-character prefix, invite members through a link, and confirm or cancel before joining. Members can leave, and creators can manage their clan.
  • Fixed the desktop My account layout. Account navigation shows Sign in / Sign up when signed out and My account when signed in.
  • Added Privacy, Cookies and Terms pages, plus a choice to load embedded YouTube videos.

15 October 2026

Profiles and navigation

  • Shareable profiles for REL members and ranked editors, with contributions and ranking placements.
  • Contributor names on the homepage link to their profiles.
  • Contributors are grouped into Owners, Developers, Reviewers and Rankers.
  • Updated account and action icons, homepage buttons and contributor controls.
  • Navigation buttons reveal labels on hover or keyboard focus where supported; homepage and form buttons keep their labels visible.
  • Fixed buttons shifting or glitching when hovered.
  • Added animations for editor cards and supported dropdowns, with reduced-motion support.

14 October 2026

Accounts and applications

  • Sign up with a username, confirm your email and recover a forgotten password.
  • Change your username once every 7 days.
  • Edit your linked editor profile from My account, or request a link to an existing profile.
  • Choose your editing styles when applying, including an Other option.
  • Profile edits save automatically, and rankings refresh as changes are published.
