Updates to Roblox Editors List.

15 October 2026 · Upcoming

Profiles and navigation

  • Shareable profiles for REL members and ranked editors, with contributions and ranking placements.
  • Contributor names on the homepage link to their profiles.
  • Contributors are grouped into Owners, Developers, Reviewers and Rankers.
  • New account and action icons, with a green gradient for My account.
  • Top navigation icons expand to reveal their labels on hover or keyboard focus.
  • Homepage actions are centered, with Sign in / Sign up in the header and a shorter Apply button.
  • Sliding icon buttons extend across list and account navigation, with animated contributor and role checkboxes.
  • Sign-in and sign-up controls stay visible, with clearer password recovery and button spacing.
  • Arrows and outlined buttons replace underlined profile links.
  • Editor cards slide and fade open. Filter and list dropdowns animate in supported browsers.
  • Action labels stay visible on touchscreens, with reduced-motion support for animations.

14 October 2026

Accounts and applications

  • Sign up with a username, confirm your email and recover a forgotten password.
  • Change your username once every 7 days.
  • Edit your linked editor profile from My account, or request a link to an existing profile.
  • Choose your editing styles when applying, including an Other option.
  • Profile edits save automatically, and rankings refresh as changes are published.
