# REL security and privacy interface review

Reading this as: existing REL pages gaining privacy information, visitor control over showcase players and accessible staff form labels. During mode remains the user's explicit session choice. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. Retain REL's dark green surfaces, Red Hat Text, logo and existing action icons. Policies use a readable prose column; the showcase notice occupies the existing video area. No new logo, image, team information or marketing claim is created.

## Block 1: Hard Gate

PASS. Privacy, Cookies and Terms use actual REL account/application behaviour, the owner's confirmed UK operation and approved public contact. They do not claim certification, guaranteed deletion deadlines, a company address or a paid service. The new copy contains no em dashes, invented reviews or unsupported usage statistics. Existing content outside this change was not rewritten.

PASS. Chromium checked the three policy pages and both video views at 320, 390, 768 and 1440px with genuine local Red Hat Text fixtures: no horizontal overflow or font mismatch. Screenshots of the mobile privacy page, public-profile notice and ranking-drawer notice were inspected. Layouts keep readable paragraphs and a visible player action at the narrowest width. The existing account, application and admin layouts also passed the browser regression runner.

PASS. The new action and policy navigation have at least 44px control heights. The player can be loaded by Enter or click; closing a ranking drawer with Escape removes its frame and reopening restores the notice. Before a choice there is no YouTube frame or thumbnail request. The requested YouTube frame URL was intercepted with a local response, so actual third-party playback is not claimed as tested. Static policy links have visible focus outlines, and the published email is a real mailto destination supplied by the owner.

PASS. The contrast script measured primary text on the policy background at 18.22:1, muted text at 10.94:1, green links at 14.32:1, player notice text at 10.40:1 and button text on green at 13.64:1. The player boundary measures 4.15:1, above the 3:1 non-text requirement. Focus outlines use the tested green or foreground colour. No theme switch was introduced.

PASS. Admin editor and site inputs have associated labels; style fields have accessible names. Browser checks inspect these associations. Signup/application policy notices link to built pages, and the existing application permission checkbox remains required. Error/loading states and existing form actions remain exercised by the regression runner. CSP rejection of injected script was tested separately from the existing safe-text rendering test.

## Block 2: Purpose-Gate

PASS. The prose column keeps policy reading within a useful line length. Section headings group account data, public data, providers, retention and requests around visitors' questions. Green remains REL's existing link/action accent. No new gradients, decorative icons, artwork, badges, glows or motion were added. The plain player button names the service it loads; its notice explains the consequence before that connection happens.

## Block 3: Liveliness

PASS. The established logo and green links preserve REL's identity. Policy titles lead each reading page; headings and paragraph spacing provide its rhythm. In showcase areas, the green load control is the focal action. Existing navigation motion is preserved, and new policy/player controls keep labels visible. The declared low-motion, reading-focused direction matches the result.

## Block 4: Craftsmanship and Quality Locks

PASS. The site build includes all three policy pages and both shared player files. `npm test` passed all 15 test files; Functions compile and actual local Wrangler responses retain the intended headers. The extended Chromium runner completed account/recovery, staff roles, ranking/application flows, autosave, disclosure and new privacy/player checks without runtime exceptions. It uses fictional data and does not send real emails.

PASS. The public contact is owner-approved and policy details are tied to inspected code. The security guide records provider/retention settings that still need operator verification, rather than declaring legal compliance. No production data, rankings, `data.js`, existing application roadmap or unrelated screen design was changed. Release prerequisites are documented and this review does not authorise production SQL or deployment.
